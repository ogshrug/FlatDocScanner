# Play Store listing (draft)

**App name** (30 characters max)
FlatDocScanner: Book Scanner

**Short description** (80 characters max)
Scan books and documents into flat, clean PDFs. Works offline, no account.

**Full description**

Turn your phone into a book scanner. Hold it over an open book, turn the pages, and get flat,
clean, readable pages, without tapping.

**Scans that look scanned**
• Finds the page automatically, even on busy desks
• Flattens curved book pages with on-device AI
• Book mode splits an open book into two pages
• Removes fingers holding the page down and the shadow in the gutter
• Colour, grayscale and black & white looks

**Hands-free**
• Auto-capture when you hold still, and again after you turn the page
• Interval timer for steady page-turning
• Flash for dim rooms

**Text, when you want it**
• Recognise text on one page or a whole document (English and Hindi scripts)
• Search inside your scans, with matches marked on the page
• Read aloud
• Export as searchable PDF, Word, TXT or Markdown

**Organised**
• Folders with covers, search and sorting
• Reorder, rotate, crop, move pages between folders
• Share as PDF, images or ZIP, or save to your gallery
• Trash with 30-day restore

**Private by design**
Everything happens on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking, and the app has no internet
access at all, so your documents never leave your device unless you share them.

**Category**: Productivity
**Tags**: document scanner, book scanner, PDF scanner, OCR
